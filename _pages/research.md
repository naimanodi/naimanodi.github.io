### Research Assistant at the Trier Center for Digital Humanities (TCDH), Universität Trier: (Oct 2025 - Jul 2026)
My role as a Research Assistant at the TCDH under the DFG funded project [Modernity as Loss?](https://tcdh.uni-trier.de/en/projekt/modernity-loss) involves Exploring narratives of decline and cultural critique in European literature (German). The task also comprises, investigating on the quantitative and qualitative approach to large literary corpora, supporting computational modeling of semantic and evaluative features using word embedding. The work of this project have been submitted to the [Digital Humanities 2027](https://dh2027.adho.org/) for publication.

### Research Assistant Computerlinguistik und Digital Humanities, Universität Trier: (May 2025 - Sept 2025)
- The [xPrEs](https://www.uni-trier.de/fileadmin/fb2/LDV/CL/Dokumente/Team/Werner-Rettinger-Crossmodal-perception-trace.pdf) project included the analysis of multimodal data recorded via iMotions. 🥽 I have worked on a novel content representation paradigm for multimedia documents (images and texts) by modeling sequences of attention shifts across multiple modalities, investigating and analyzing similarities and differences between human perceptual patterns and those learned by state-of-the-art representation learning techniques. 📊

### Research Assistant - Anglistik, Universität Trier: (Aug 2024 - Jan 2025)
I have worked as a Research Assistant in the Anglistik Department at the Universität Trier, focusing on the field of Speaker Diarization and machine speaker identification. 🎙️
Contributed to the Mismatch Condition in Machine Speaker Identification project, where I explore ways to improve recognition accuracy in challenging acoustic conditions. 👭
Explored machine learning models, integrating feature extraction and noise robustness techniques to enhance speaker recognition performance across diverse acoustic environments. 🔍
The work has been published in the Problems of Forensic Sciences Journal under the title [Challenges for Auditory and Automatic Speaker Recognition: Evaluating Cases of Highly Similar Voices](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1394719)
